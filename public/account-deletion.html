SmartStack Solutions Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your SmartStack Solutions account and associated app data by contacting support.

How to request deletion

Send an email from the address connected to your SmartStack Solutions account to: smartstacksolutions@gmail.com

Use the subject line: Account deletion request.

What data is deleted

We will delete account-associated app data stored for bills, spending, income, notifications, and savings targets.

Processing time

Account deletion requests are reviewed and processed within a reasonable period after we verify account ownership.
